# Demo artwork

Pictures shown by the public demo’s media widgets. All are freely licensed:

- Films and series: open movies by [Blender Studio](https://studio.blender.org/films/), Creative Commons Attribution (CC BY).
- Books: covers of [Standard Ebooks](https://standardebooks.org) editions, dedicated to the public domain (CC0).
- Comics: [Pepper&Carrot](https://www.peppercarrot.com) by David Revoy, CC BY 4.0.
- Photos: [NASA](https://images.nasa.gov), public domain (NASA does not endorse this project).

They were resized and cropped for the demo; nothing else was changed.
