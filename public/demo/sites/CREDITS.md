# Demo bookmark icons

The public demo's bookmarks point to real sites. Each file here is that site's
own published icon (its touch icon or logo), named after the site's host, and
shown only next to a link to that site, as a browser shows a site's icon. The
icons and the names they carry remain the trademarks of their owners; they are
not covered by NasDash's licence.
